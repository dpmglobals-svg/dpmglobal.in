Who We Are & What We Do

DPM Global is an India-based international export-import enterprise and real estate advisory firm. Founded in 2007, we have spent over 18 years building robust supply chain networks and strategic real estate land developments.

Our core commercial operations span two primary industry verticals:

Agricultural Exports

Direct sourcing, export grading, and shipping of fresh onions, basmati rice, potatoes, garlic, ginger, table eggs, green chillies, and authentic Indian spices to buyers worldwide.

Real Estate Plotting

Advisory and strategic land development focusing on high-ROI commercial, industrial, and residential plotting in key industrial growth hubs of Sonipat and Kharkhoda.

Regulatory Assistance

Assisting corporate clients and trade partners in fulfilling essential government compliance, import-export filings, and documentation requirements smoothly.
