Our Certificates

We are a verified, authorized, and compliant organization. Here are our official registrations and global standard certificates that prove our genuinity.

Authorized by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority. Ensures our exports meet strict international quality guidelines.

GST Certificate

Fully compliant with the Goods and Services Tax framework of India. A testament to our transparent and legal domestic trading footprint.

IEC (Import Export Code)

Issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT). This validates our authority to legally ship and import goods across international borders.

FSSAI License

Certified by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. Guarantees that our agricultural products are 100% safe, hygienic, and consumable.

Proudly registered as an authorized exporter of premium and authentic Indian spices to markets worldwide.

ISO 9001:2015

Internationally recognized certification for Quality Management Systems. We maintain high standards in processing, packaging, and delivery.
